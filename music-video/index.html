FAQ

Good to know

How long does a session take? +

Most sessions run two to four hours depending on the package. One focused session is enough — that is the whole point.

What do I need to bring? +

Your finished track (or a rough mix), one or two outfits, and yourself. We handle camera, lighting, direction and post.

Do I need dance experience? +

Not at all. A director guides your movement on the day, and the edit is built around the strongest moments.

Where in London do you film? +

Anywhere that fits the track — rooftops, neon streets, tunnels, estates, studios and landmarks. We help you choose.

How fast is the turnaround? +

A first cut typically lands within 48 hours of the session, with final delivery shortly after revisions.

Who owns the video? +

You do. You get full rights to the finished master and the raw footage, and you can release it wherever you like.
